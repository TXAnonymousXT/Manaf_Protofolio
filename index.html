About the object

There is no such switch, and this is no photograph of one. It was sculpted in code as a distance field, the way glaze softens a casting, down to the jacks, vents and fan blades. A radial fracture from one point of impact then cut it into 96 watertight pieces, each with its own broken face.

Every frame is drawn live in your browser, about 340,000 trianglesNormally it is drawn live in the browser; these are stills of it, in low sun through a window, with softboxes to catch the glaze, as a photographer would light porcelain. The maker's mark on the lid is my name. The plaques around it are drawn the same way, and they break the same way.
